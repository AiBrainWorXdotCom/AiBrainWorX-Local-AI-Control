# Model licensing and downloads

The repository's MIT license covers AiBrainWorX's original code, not third-party model weights or software.

For the specific model in our article:

- [ISTA-DASLab Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-GSQ-RCO-Coder-GGUF model card](https://huggingface.co/ISTA-DASLab/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-GSQ-RCO-Coder-GGUF) declares Apache-2.0 and identifies Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next as its base.
- [Upstream Qwen3.8-Flash-Next LICENSE](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next/blob/main/LICENSE) is Qwen Community License 1.0. It includes conditions on commercial Model-as-a-Service and AI Work Assistant businesses, and attribution conditions above stated usage/revenue thresholds.

The difference between those declarations should be clarified with the relevant licensors before relying on unrestricted redistribution or commercial coding-assistant use. We do not resolve it by relabeling the model MIT. Downloading from upstream does not remove applicable model terms.

Setup keeps downloads separate and requires confirmation before large model acquisition. If you choose another model in Strata, inspect that exact model's provenance and license as well. No model weights are bundled here.

License information was checked on October 24, 2026. Review upstream terms again for the version you obtain. A user confirmation in setup is not a promise that every downstream use is permitted.

## The model library

The 36 text suggestions include 33 MIT/Apache-2.0 references and three gated Gemma checkpoints with their own Gemma terms. The wider 56-profile catalog includes voice, vision, embeddings and video references. Each
entry keeps the creator, original model ID, source URL and reviewed revision.
AiBrainWorX brands its launcher, memory and presets; it does not claim authorship
of upstream model weights. Suggested use cases and hardware tiers are editorial
guidance rather than tested compatibility.

Specialized and research profiles can carry other licenses, prominently identified. Pocket-TTS lists attribution terms; MobileNet is reference-only pending exact weight-license review. Gemma access is never accepted on the user’s behalf. Datasets and auxiliary voice/video assets have their own rights and are not bundled. For
example, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 declares MIT; Qwen3.8 Flash Next, Kimi K3 and
Cosmos3-H-Dreams have custom terms. No weights from any of these are included.

Before redistributing a downloaded model or derivative, check its exact license,
base-model requirements and required notices. A permissive software license for
an inference engine does not automatically license its models.
